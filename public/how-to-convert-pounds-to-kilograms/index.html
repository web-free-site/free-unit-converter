How to convert pounds to kilograms

Pounds and kilograms are both units of mass in everyday use. International kits and many official forms use kilograms; some household scales still show pounds. Free Unit Converter can switch between them in the mass category.

  1. Open the mass converter. On Free Unit Converter, choose the mass (weight) category or the mass page.
  2. Select pounds and kilograms. Set one list to pounds (lb) and the other to kilograms (kg).
  3. Enter the value and read the result. Type the number you have. You can also type in the other field to convert in the opposite direction, or use the swap control.
  4. Optional: the factor. One international pound is 0.45359237 kilograms by definition. The converter uses standard factors like this rather than a rough guess.

Mass versus force

In everyday language people say "weight" for both. This tool is converting mass units (lb and kg), which is what bathroom scales and shipping forms usually mean.

Stone and ounces

If you need stone or ounces as well, pick those units in the same mass category instead of doing a second manual calculation.
